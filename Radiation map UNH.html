How it works

Each square is 10 m on a side (about 33 ft), roughly a medium classroom. The grid is fixed to the ground, so a square always covers the same spot.

Phone GPS is usually good to 5–10 m outdoors and much worse indoors or beside tall buildings. When the chip in the corner turns yellow or red, check the blue dashed square against the map before you save.
